“Meeting the urgent needs of society without breaching the Earth’s ecological boundaries demands a radical paradigm shift in how we conceive and build.”
Andrew Grant • Founding Director

Artasian recognises the dual climate and biodiversity emergency as the defining civilizational challenge of our era. As landscape architects, we hold the profound responsibility—and unique capability—to shape how humanity inhabits the earth in harmony with nature.

As a founding signatory of UK Landscape Architects Declare, we align our entire studio practice with regenerative design principles. Rather than simply minimizing harm, our masterplans act as active ecological engines: purifying water runoffs, capturing atmospheric carbon, cooling overheated metropolitan heat islands, and cultivating rich wildlife corridors.

From the iconic biodiverse superstructures of Gardens by the Bay in Singapore to restorative green corridors across the United Kingdom, our architectural interventions reconnect dense urban communities with the vital rhythms of the natural world.

Strategic Roadmap

Our 5 Pillars of Action

Concrete protocols implemented across every feasibility study, masterplan, and completed landscape project.

01 / SEQUESTRATION

Design for Net-Zero Carbon

Prioritising carbon sequestration through engineered living soils, deep-root mature tree canopies, and rigorously specified low-embodied carbon masonry, dry-stone, and sustainably harvested timber.

Lifecycle Analysis Active Protocol
02 / HABITAT

Biodiversity Net Gain (>20%)

Surpassing statutory minimums by engineering multi-layered indigenous planting palettes that serve as contiguous ecological highways for pollinators, birds, and native flora across high-density cityscapes.

Ecological Metric Active Protocol
03 / HYDROLOGY

Water-Sensitive Urban Design

Transforming stormwater from a flood hazard into a vibrant civic asset. Integrating bio-filtration swales, permeable pavements, and cascading rain gardens that clean and replenish subterranean aquifers.

Closed-Loop SuDS Active Protocol
04 / CIRCULARITY

Hyper-Local Material Stewardship

Specifying quarried stone, aggregates, and nursery stock within immediate regional radii. Mandating high-durability reclaimed elements and whole-life disassembly plans for zero-landfill construction.

Regional Sourcing Active Protocol
05 / CIVIC WELLBEING

Restoring the Biophilic Connection

Groundbreaking neuroscience proves that daily visual and tactile contact with living landscapes drastically lowers cardiovascular stress and fosters community cohesion. Every Artasian design ensures that all citizens—regardless of background—enjoy unhindered daily immersion in therapeutic, sensorial nature.

Social & Mental Health Active Protocol
