ABOUT ARTASIAN BIOPHILIC LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE

Designing Experiences Beyond Spaces

At Artasian, we believe landscape architecture is not merely about ornamentation—it is an intimate dialogue between living ecology, human emotional wellbeing, and timeless built form.

From private estate courtyards in the Mediterranean to landmark urban sanctuaries across London and Singapore, our multi-disciplinary studio unites visionary landscape design with regenerative engineering. We craft enduring outdoor environments that mature gracefully across generations.

Architectural Precision

Harmonizing structure, water features, and artisan stonework with mathematical poise.

Regenerative Ecology

Zero-waste water filtration, microclimate buffering, and native biodiversity corridors.

Specifying Resilient Tree Canopies for a Changing Climate

Confronting climate extremes requires rigorous botanical science. Surveying mature arboretums across Europe and Southeast Asia, our research team isolates drought-hardy, high-canopy native species to mitigate urban heat islands and safeguard regional biodiversity.

  • ✓ Longitudinal climate stress testing on native tree specimens
